Leonida Trophy Guide

All 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze — with a guide for each one, a checklist that saves in your browser, and a roadmap to the platinum. Works fully offline: nothing is fetched from the network.

Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 at about 200 hours (Push Square says up to 300 hours or more). One playthrough is enough: all 55 trophies are offline and single-player, there are no difficulty settings, and free-roam continues after the story. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off. Only one trophy is possibly missable — Positive Mental Attitude — see its flag below.
